## Expected
Like `deploy` and `status` when the target is locked: say what was waited for (which table, which session/pid), that nothing was written, and what to do (wait, or raise `lockTimeout` for the target, which the README says applies to `init --adopt`).

### Triage, 2026-10-21

**Verification:** CONFIRMED. On `qa2t_adopt_lock`, another session held `ACCESS EXCLUSIVE` on `app.t`. Three adoptions each failed after the default 5 s lock timeout with `Error: pg-delta could not diff the schemas: canceling statement due to lock timeout`. Nothing was written, and no history schema was created.

**Cause:** adoption's `readTarget` (`commands/adopt.ts`) applies the target's `lock_timeout` to its read session. It doesn't catch the resulting 55P03 from:
- the differ, which wraps it in `DifferError` (`differ/pg-delta.ts`, naming pg-delta);
- `catalogFacts`, `columnOrder` and the other queries, which pass the raw error through.

Deployments already add a next step for 55P03 and 57014 (`SqlFileFailedError`, `nextStep` in `rehearsal/replay.ts`). Adoption has nothing like it.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** When adoption's read of the target runs out of lockTimeout or statementTimeout, it refuses with a message saying what happened, that nothing was written, and what to do.

**Current behavior:**
A lock held on a managed-schema table makes `init --adopt` fail with a raw `canceling statement due to lock timeout`, sometimes prefixed with `pg-delta could not diff the schemas:`. A user has never heard of pg-delta, and the message doesn't say that nothing was written.

**Desired behavior:**
- A lock timeout (SQLSTATE 55P03) or statement timeout (57014) anywhere in adoption's read of the target gives a typed adoption refusal, e.g. `Cannot adopt target qa: reading its managed schema waited longer than its lockTimeout (5s) for a lock another session holds. Nothing was written; retry once that session has finished, or raise lockTimeout for target qa in super-deploy.config.ts`.
- The duration is the target's configured value, not the default.
- The message never names pg-delta.
- Other errors from the read are unchanged.

**Key interfaces:**
- The adoption command's target-reading step, and `DifferError` (its `cause` carries the SQLSTATE).
- A new `SuperDeployError` subclass, or a new `AdoptConflictError` reason, exported from the index.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] API seam: with a test session holding `ACCESS EXCLUSIVE` on a managed table and the target's `lockTimeout` at `200ms`, `adopt` rejects with the typed error. Its message names the timeout's configured value and says nothing was written. The project has no new files, and the target has no history schema.
- [ ] The same with the lock held only during the differ's read: no `pg-delta` in the message.
- [ ] Adopting after the lock is released succeeds.

**Out of scope:**
- Naming the session that holds the lock (146).
- Timeout messages elsewhere quoting the default instead of the configured value (145).
- Ctrl+C during the wait (134).


- To find the difference, you have to compare two long lists by eye.
- `-` turned out to be the target's actual state and `+` what the migrations expect. Nothing says so, and in a diff `-` usually reads as "removed". A hand-made *grant* shows up on the `-` side, which looks like a removal.
- Quoted role names come out double-escaped: `{"\"adopt_Reporter\":USAGE/<owner>", ...}`.
- Tables are shown without their schema (`table orders`), while the summary line says `schema app (changed), table orders (changed)`.
